REELFORGE · FIRST RELEASE

Made to move
with your music.

This build creates a full 60-second animated music video with multiple visual scenes, transitions, typography and audio-reactive motion. You can blend in your own footage. It renders locally in your browser and exports WebM. It does not pretend to generate AI camera footage or transcribe lyrics; those require external AI services and are not silently simulated.
