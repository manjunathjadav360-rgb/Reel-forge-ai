REELFORGE · FIRST RELEASE

Made to move
with your music.

This build creates a 60-second vertical music video from an uploaded song video or audio. When you upload a video, its original footage and soundtrack are used together; you can also add optional extra footage. It adds transitions, audio-reactive motion and optional lyric-synced text. Paste lyrics to sequence each line and guide visual moods. It renders locally and exports MP4. With a Gemini API key, it analyses audio, transcribes lyrics and creates an automatic visual plan. It does not generate original live-action footage.
